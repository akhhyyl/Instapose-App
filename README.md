# Instapose-App

<H2> Instapose App Privacy Policy </h2>

"Instapose" is a free photography app that helps users discover and recreate different photography poses using AI recommendations, pose templates, camera features, and visual pose overlays.

The app allows users to create an account and use additional features associated with their account.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decided to use our service.


<H3> Collecting Information </H3>

Instapose allows users to create an account.

Depending on the account creation method used, we may collect information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Profile information
- Authentication information
- Account-related identifiers

This information is used to create and manage your InstaPose account and provide account-related features.

Instapose uses Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication, application functionality, data storage, and other backend services.

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information collected through Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

<H3> Photos and Videos </H3>

InstaPose may request access to photos and videos when you choose features such as:

- Selecting a photo or video from your device
- Adding a photo as a pose reference
- Using a photo as a pose template overlay
- Taking photographs
- Recording videos
- Using camera-based pose guidance

Photos and videos are accessed only when required for a feature selected by the user.

Where supported, InstaPose may use the Android system Photo Picker to allow users to select specific photos or videos without granting access to their entire media library.

We do not access your photos or videos in the background.

<H3> Camera Usage </H3>

InstaPose uses the device camera to provide photography and video features, including pose templates, pose overlays, camera guidance, and photo/video capture.

Camera access is activated only when the user chooses a camera-related feature.

InstaPose does not use the camera in the background.


<H3> AI Features </H3>

InstaPose provides AI-powered features such as pose recommendations, pose guidance, and template recommendations.

AI features may process information from selected photos, videos, pose templates, or camera-based features to provide the requested recommendations.

AI processing is used to provide photography and posing assistance.

InstaPose does not use users' photos or videos for advertising purposes.


<H3> Firebase and Third-Party Services </H3>

InstaPose uses Firebase services provided by Google.

Firebase may be used for services including:

- User authentication
- Account management
- Cloud data storage
- Application backend functionality
- Crash reporting and diagnostics, if enabled
- Analytics, if enabled

The specific Firebase services used by InstaPose may change as the application develops.

Firebase and Google services may process certain technical or account-related information according to their applicable privacy policies.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy


<H3> Content Usage </H3>

The pose images displayed in Instapose are curated from publicly available sources, primarily Pinterest.

These images are posted by real people and may include faces or identifiable features.

Instapose does not claim ownership of any images shown in the app.

All credits and rights belong to the original creators or photographers.

Images are used solely for educational and reference purposes to guide photography poses.

If you are the rightful owner of any image used and want it removed, please contact us via the email below. We will promptly remove the content upon verification.
